# Packaged rotations

Definitions in this folder are addon data and read-only in game. They always appear in the rotation list, including disabled and inapplicable definitions. Enable preferences live in the profile's `rotationLibrary.preferences`. Clone creates an independent per-spec custom body in the existing SavedVariables rotation store; `rotationLibrary.origins` keeps its manifest, source revision, baseline and step origins. The default is never copied into that editable store automatically. Editing a clone cannot alter its source. Changing profiles rebinds preferences; no source pack overwrites saved rotations or the user's existing fallback.

The first pack is **TBC Shaman early leveling, levels 1–29**, a preview of the authoring/runtime path, not the completed all-class corpus. It uses independent optional highlights for missing weapon imbue and Lightning Shield, plus a Lightning Bolt opener, Earth Shock and a stationary ranged Lightning Bolt filler in the sequential priority. Missing upkeep never blocks a damage recommendation. Known-spell guards skip untrained abilities. Actions use the existing rank expansion/highlighting path; the portable numeric Lightning Shield predicate opts into readable localized rank-family matching. Existing temporary weapon enchants are respected. Spell usability/resources/range and actual action-bar availability are checked by the engine; this pack does not cast actions automatically.

This deliberately modest starter does not encode healing/interrupt overrides, consumables, totem placement, movement-aware openers, enemy time-to-live, Flame Shock weaving, automatic selection of the five-second-rule refresh window (upkeep remains an optional reminder), or later talents/spec rotations. It stops applying at level 30. Revision3 adds the requested ranged filler: native Attack range is preferred; if unavailable, a conservative LibRangeCheck five-yard interval is used. An uncertain interval never becomes a positive beyond-melee match. Normal spell range/usability still applies to the suggested Bolt. This ranged adaptation is RM behavior requested during live testing, rather than a claim that the source guide prescribes that exact decision graph. Do not extrapolate it to max level, dungeons, PvP or other editions. Those remain separate sourced corpus work. Source review and offline scenarios do not establish live recommendation quality or optimal DPS. In-game leveling/rank/highlight verification remains outstanding.

Its guidance was checked against [Icy Veins' original Enhancement leveling guide](https://www.icy-veins.com/tbc-classic/enhancement-shaman-leveling-guide) on 2026-10-20 (page revision 2026-01-12). The implementation adapts the guide's upkeep, pull and shock priorities into a small starter. Spell identity/rank evidence is recorded in [the authoring evidence](../docs/quality/default-spell-evidence.md); the manifest cites the rotation guide. No guide prose or complete guide was reproduced. Shield ranks are matched through actual learned state and readable aura identities. An assumed training level never makes an action available.

Authoring requirements: version 1 envelope, stable pack ID/revision and step IDs, explicit edition/action context, localized name/description keys, provenance and independent condition tables. Class/spec may be omitted for shared packs. Unknown condition/schema/edition data remains visible but unavailable for execution. Roles, intent and ruleset selectors currently remain reference-only until their context providers are implemented; do not silently treat them as universal. Retail/Forever defaults remain reference-only under the current restricted-data policy.

Run `python3 tools/check`. `tests/defaults.lua` loads real predicates and the shipped pack, checks structural resolution/localization/source scope, and exercises untrained abilities, upkeep, both shield ranks, combat and friendly targets. This is the starting fixture for a broader corpus scenario suite. The shared native roster/editor now shows packaged and custom definitions with the same read-only/editable card layout.

`tests/default_corpus.lua` automatically loads every direct `Defaults/` Lua entry in the TOC across five synthetic client families and thirteen class tokens. It checks source/review metadata, English name/description coverage, schema resolution on matching editions/classes, nested stable IDs, definition validation, visibility, read-only isolation, enable/disable and runtime exclusion at sampled level/spec/build/context boundaries. Invalid-manifest probes verify the guard rejects missing translations/sources, invalid dates and incomplete conditions/branches. It never evaluates combat conditions. This is an authoring/selection gate; it adds neither class coverage nor gameplay scenarios, and synthetic declarations do not certify live APIs or recommendation quality. Add pack-specific behavioral scenarios alongside new packs.

Default, clone and import validation share effective global/profile item-set references, with a nonnil profile entry taking precedence. Dependency additions/removals and registered schema changes refresh catalog validation without reload; unchanged lookups do not revalidate all packaged graphs. A structurally valid packaged source remains visible with diagnostics if a changed condition schema makes it invalid. Runtime eligibility keeps that source inert; custom saves still use strict current-schema validation. Extensions should change schemas through RegisterCondition/UnregisterCondition rather than silently mutating nested fields.

The second pack is **TBC Mage early Frost leveling, levels1–29**, revision3. Its original priority data adapts [Icy Veins' single-target Frost leveling guide](https://www.icy-veins.com/tbc-classic/frost-single-target-mage-leveling-guide) (reviewed2026-10-20, guide revision2026-01-12): optional missing Intellect/armor, then filler preferences selected by trained early ranks. Base action families use the existing ranked lookup: the highest learned, mapped and ready candidate is preferred; the engine may fall back to a lower rank within the selected family. Each training-dependent arm contains exactly one filler. An unavailable chosen spell does not select the opposite arm. One shared stationary/hostile target guard allows combat or an unengaged pull target, so there is no repeated pull/combat tree or redundant Stop. These choices adapt the guide rather than calculate damage. Upkeep accepts other casters and localized higher ranks.

This Frost-oriented preview omits kiting, crowd control, emergency shields, finishers, wand optimization, consumables, talent cooldowns, mana management, Fire/Arcane talent-specific priorities and levels30+. It does not establish optimal DPS. `tests/defaults_mage.lua` runs real predicates/readiness through early/late training, availability, movement/target state and independent upkeep, plus multi-step traversal through the real sequence executor, spell catalog and main rank expansion. No guide prose or complete guide was copied. Broader class/spec/role/edition defaults and client verification remain pending.

Revision5 supersedes revision4's optional Attack reminder. Weapon Attack is now a universal implicit final sequential fallback: after no earlier action qualifies, in living hostile melee combat with inactive autoattack and all normal action checks satisfied. The engine never inserts a saved step or operates a button. A matching Stop ends lower priorities; the fallback remains eligible unless explicitly suppressed. An unavailable Stop guard suppresses both. Optional upkeep remains independent. Explicit same-action known checks were removed from this pack because every spell action now requires learned state, resources, cooldown and range automatically. Cross-action checks remain available. Existing clones retain their authored steps; compare against the current source to remove an older optional Attack if desired. Actual Attack-button identification/highlighting remains a client test.

Revision6 adds a Stop-if-not-in-combat boundary immediately after the pull opener. Combat priorities below it no longer repeat the player-combat predicate. Stop permits the implicit melee fallback unless its new suppression option is enabled; the fallback still independently requires player combat, so it cannot propose a new pull after the pre-combat boundary. Existing clones retain their prior authored conditions.

Revision7 replaces the two explicit Lightning Shield rank checks with one `BUFF` predicate using `spell=324, anyrank=true`. Exact numeric-ID aura checks remain the default elsewhere. The opt-in compares readable localized names after the exact-ID check, so it can recognize a higher rank without English literals or enumerating all ranks. Unreadable metadata or aura identity stays unavailable, including under NOT. This does not expand the pack beyond levels1–19 or certify later-level recommendations.

Built In composition is planned as authoring-time reuse of substantial named blocks, with edition/spec overrides and explicit dependency revisions. TBC may share Classic blocks only after reviewing changed talents and mechanics; shared spell names alone are insufficient. Expansion must reject cycles, missing blocks, duplicate IDs and incompatible editions. The runtime, editor, export and clone should consume a fully expanded independent tree: cloning copies every included step, and editing or updating its source cannot modify the clone. Full TBC Frost/Fire leveling packs (1–70, adapting to known spells/talents) remain corpus work; the current 1–29 Mage pack is an explicitly bounded preview.

Shaman revision8 moves individual spell-record evidence into the authoring document; its action tree is unchanged from revision7. Mage revision2 also removes the duplicate pull/combat tree and opposite-family readiness fallbacks.

Shaman revision9 and Mage revision3 extend the existing previews to levels1–29 without adding abilities or changing their step IDs, priorities or optional upkeep. Learned ranks, native readiness and bar mappings still decide availability; reaching a level never implies a trainer visit. Mage retains Frost Armor through this bracket; Ice Armor is a separate later family. Icy Veins and other talent/utility decisions remain manual. These bounded continuations are not complete talent builds or optimal-DPS claims. `tests/tbc_twenty_defaults.lua` exercises the real catalog, sequence and readiness with modeled twenty-level ranks, missing mappings/resources, cooldowns, unavailable observations and localized upkeep. Current Built In sources and new clones receive the wider scope. Existing Shaman revision8 and Mage revision2 clones retain their independent earlier cutoff and custom priorities until an explicit source update or fresh clone; no saved rotation is rewritten automatically. Warlock revision2 separately extends to1–29 with the armor supersession policy below.

A shared leveling pack is appropriate when removing unavailable spells from its later-level priorities leaves the correct early priority order. A change in relative order due to talents or mechanics requires an explicit conditional branch or a distinct automatically selected pack; spell availability alone must not be assumed to prove equivalence.

The third pack is **TBC Warlock early Affliction leveling, levels 1–29**, revision2 (armor policy reviewed2026-10-20). Its original priority data adapts [Icy Veins’ Affliction leveling guide](https://www.icy-veins.com/tbc-classic/affliction-warlock-leveling-guide) (reviewed2026-10-18, guide revision2026-01-12). It maintains the player’s Immolate, Corruption and Curse of Agony across learned ranks, then offers Drain Life below70% health during combat or a stationary wand/Shadow Bolt fallback. The70% threshold is an RM starter heuristic, rather than a threshold asserted by the guide. Native learned state, resources, cooldowns, range and bar mapping govern recommendations; a missing wand does not block a ready Bolt. Another caster’s DoT does not count as the player’s own. Optional upkeep prefers learned Demon Armor (706 family) over Demon Skin (687 family). Once Demon Armor is known, missing bar mapping, cooldown or unavailable readiness does not authorize a Demon Skin fallback. An active Demon Armor aura also suppresses Demon Skin; unavailable observations remain unavailable. This is an explicit RM policy for these mutually exclusive armor families.

A matching channel Stop preserves an ongoing channel; a learned-wand auto-repeat Stop prevents recommending Shoot as an off-toggle. DoTs and low-health Drain Life intentionally have priority over continuing wanding. An unavailable auto-repeat observation halts lower priorities; an unavailable individual aura predicate skips its own DoT instead of treating unknown as absent. A final suppress-Attack Stop prevents a caster from receiving implicit melee advice when ranged actions are exhausted. Normal target guards permit deliberate pulls but avoid out-of-combat advice against an already engaged target.

Demon control/summoning, Life Tap, shard management, Drain Soul finishers, healthstones, curses other than Agony, fears, multi-target play, time-to-live estimates, wand damage optimization, talent-specific movement and levels30+ remain manual or omitted. Corruption conservatively requires standing still even if Improved Corruption has made it instant; training or talents are never inferred from level. This bounded preview does not establish optimal DPS or live recommendation quality. `tests/defaults_warlock.lua` exercises real predicates, the owned aura adapter, rank expansion, sequence stops and native readiness, with a synthetic bar-mapping boundary. Native leveling/highlight acceptance remains outstanding. No guide prose or full guide is reproduced.

Existing Warlock revision1 clones retain their independent level19 cutoff and armor policy until explicit source adoption or a fresh clone. `tests/defaults_warlock_armor.lua` covers the real publisher, learned/owned aura state, readiness and mapping boundaries, plus unchanged archived clones. No saved custom rotation is upgraded automatically.

The fourth pack is **TBC Priest early leveling, levels1–19**, revision1. Native Priest gameplay acceptance remains outstanding.
