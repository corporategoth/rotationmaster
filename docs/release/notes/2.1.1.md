## Fixed

- **Metrics are per character.** The Metrics tab only reports the character you are logged in as: adherence, reaction time, deviations, output and forecast accuracy no longer mix in fights from your other characters.
- **The forecast is per character.** What the forecast learns (what a spell applies, its damage and healing, item cooldowns, the base mana) is kept for the character that cast it, so it never predicts or lists a spell the character cannot cast. Preview > Forecast shows this character's list, and Forget and Ignore apply to this character.

## Upgrading

- From 2.1.0, the metrics and what the forecast had learned were saved for all your characters together and cannot be split, so they are cleared once; each character starts its own record and the forecast relearns as you play.
