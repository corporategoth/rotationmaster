## Highlights

- **Metrics.** A Metrics tab reports adherence, reaction time, deviations, output per fight and forecast accuracy, with charts, comparisons between periods, instances or rotations, and exports.
- **Talent tree window** on every client, laid out by Blizzard's own talent frame code, with ranks, a comparison and Accept.
- **Retail and Forever in combat.** More conditions keep working where the game hides combat data: moving, swing timer, combo points, totems, interruptible casts, distance, your own buffs from Blizzard's Cooldown Manager, and an opt-in "Remember hidden buffs and cooldowns". Each condition says where the game hides it.
- **Game-decided steps.** A step can be "Shown by the game when" a health or power percent is below or above a threshold; where the game hides that value it decides at draw time, in the preview and as a glow on the step's buttons. A cooldown whose only remaining condition is such a threshold becomes one automatically.
- **Cooldowns in combat.** On Retail and Forever, a cooldown that reads something the game hides in combat is left out during combat instead of guessing, and works as usual out of combat. A cooldown that waits on one of your own buffs has its announcement sound played by the game when the buff drops, in combat too.
- **New conditions:** Is a Boss, Is Elite, Unit Kind (quest boss, lieutenant, minion, trivial), Casting or Channeling, Spell Usable, Enemies Targeting You, Recent Kill, Time Until Next Swing and Macro Condition (macro conditionals such as `[combat,harm]`, evaluated by the game); boss1 to boss5 in the unit lists.
- **More action bars:** SmartTotem's totem, flyout and shield buttons, DaylightUI's bars, the shaman totem bar and Forever's gamepad bars light up, and SmartTotem and DaylightUI are listed under Integrations.
- **Ignore Moving** joins Ignore Mana and Ignore Range: by default a spell with a cast time is skipped while you move. The preview can use its own readiness options, and can show your target's health and gray out spells you cannot afford yet.
- **Forecast in the preview.** The preview predicts the recommendations after the first, with how sure it is and how many seconds each comes after the first spell, and now assumes each earlier spell was cast and worked: a pull such as Charge puts the later slots in combat (so an out-of-combat branch that ends in a Stop no longer ends the preview), a spell's cooldown, cost, charges, combo points and holy power, the buffs and debuffs it leaves (with stacks, from the game's own spell tables and what the addon sees you cast), the resource a spell gives (rage, focus) and an item's cooldown are projected. A cost the game hides is taken from the last one it showed, or from the tables.
- **Chances are guesses, and only move the confidence.** An aura seen after only some casts is a proc and is not projected; one seen most of the time is, at a cost to the confidence; a spell aimed at an enemy has a miss chance from the level difference.
- **Preview tab.** The preview window and forecast settings have their own tab after General, with an Appearance section: a background list (your class, any class, Rotation Master or a solid color), the painting shown across the page, opacity, title, target health, lock, direction and reset.
- **Class paintings.** The preview's background is a painting for each class that tells a short story across the slots and a border that fills up as icons appear. The first and last paintings run to the window edge.
- **What the forecast learned.** Preview > Forecast lists, with icons and a search, what it learned about each spell that the game's tables do not already say, and lets you forget a spell or ignore it from now on.
- **Debugging in one list.** General > Debugging is one list of every capture and report (CPU, runtime, memory, casts, glow, preview, timeline, premade spells, secret values, talents, raid marks, Lua editor) with a time, Start and Show report. Every report opens in a copyable window. `/rm debug` lists every command.

## Fixed

- The preview no longer stops showing spells after a profile switch, copy, reset or import while the bars still highlight.
- Conditions that match any rank no longer show a rank; exact-rank conditions name it.
- Shield and other own-buff checks on Forever no longer read "missing" when the game hides auras in combat.
- Long report windows show every page.
- The preview showed Charge and then nothing for a warrior out of combat.
- Forget on the forecast did nothing.
- The diagnostics list no longer shows a changed marker or enables Apply.

## Known issues

- Retail and Forever hide some combat data, and the game-decided steps, game-played sounds and cooldowns left out in combat are tested only against models of the clients. `/rm debug secrets` in combat shows what the game hides and what answered; please paste it with a report.
- The forecast's chances are guesses and only move its confidence; Retail spell-table coverage has gaps.
- Translations of the new text are AI-assisted drafts, not yet reviewed by native speakers.

## Upgrading

- From 2.0, nothing to do. From 1.x, back up your saved variables first (docs/release/BETA.md) and install every folder in the package.
- Ignore Moving is off by default, so spells with a cast time are skipped while you move; turn it on under Recommendations to have them advised anyway.
- The preview's settings moved to their own Preview tab; your choices carry over.
