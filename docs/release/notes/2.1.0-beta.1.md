## Highlights

- **Forecast in the preview.** The preview predicts the recommendations after the first, with how sure it is and how many seconds after the first spell each comes; while you cast, or while nothing is ready, it shows what comes next instead of going blank.
- **Metrics.** A Metrics tab reports adherence, reaction time, deviations, output per fight and forecast accuracy, with charts, comparisons between periods, instances or rotations, and exports.
- **Talent tree window** on every client, laid out by Blizzard's own talent frame code, with ranks, a comparison and Accept.
- **Retail and Forever in combat.** More conditions keep working where the game hides combat data: moving, swing timer, combo points, totems, interruptible casts, distance, your own buffs from Blizzard's Cooldown Manager, and an opt-in "Remember hidden buffs and cooldowns". Each condition says where the game hides it.
- **Game-decided steps.** A step can be "Shown by the game when" a health or power percent is below or above a threshold; where the game hides that value it decides at draw time, in the preview and as a glow on the step's buttons. A cooldown whose only remaining condition is such a threshold becomes one automatically.
- **Cooldowns in combat.** On Retail and Forever, a cooldown that reads something the game hides in combat is left out during combat instead of guessing, and works as usual out of combat. A cooldown that waits on one of your own buffs has its announcement sound played by the game when the buff drops, in combat too.
- **New conditions:** Is a Boss, Is Elite, Unit Kind (quest boss, lieutenant, minion, trivial), Casting or Channeling, Spell Usable, Enemies Targeting You, Recent Kill, Time Until Next Swing and Macro Condition (macro conditionals such as `[combat,harm]`, evaluated by the game); boss1 to boss5 in the unit lists.
- **Ignore Moving** joins Ignore Mana and Ignore Range: by default a spell with a cast time is skipped while you move. The preview can use its own readiness options, and can show your target's health and gray out spells you cannot afford yet.

## Fixed

- The preview no longer stops showing spells after a profile switch, copy, reset or import while the bars still highlight.
- Conditions that match any rank no longer show a rank; exact-rank conditions name it.
- Shield and other own-buff checks on Forever no longer read "missing" when the game hides auras in combat.
- Long report windows show every page.

## Known issues

- The game-decided steps, game-played sounds and cooldowns left out in combat are tested only against models of the clients. `/rm debug secrets` in combat (a dungeon too) shows what the game hides and what answered; please paste it with a report.
- Translations of the new text are AI-assisted drafts, not yet reviewed by native speakers.

## Upgrading

- From 2.0, nothing to do. From 1.x, back up your saved variables first (docs/release/BETA.md) and install every folder in the package.
- Ignore Moving is off by default, so spells with a cast time are skipped while you move; turn it on under Recommendations to have them advised anyway.
