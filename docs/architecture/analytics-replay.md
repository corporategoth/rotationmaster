# Normalized analytics replay foundation

`addon.Analytics.Replay(trace)` is a pure dot-call reducer for caller-normalized version-1 TBC/player evidence. It does not collect events, start sessions, call the recommendation engine or native APIs, save data, expose capture controls or certify native visibility/adherence. Loading the module installs no event subscriptions or timers. A future collector must separately prove its observation and presentation contracts before producing live scores.

The input header is `{format=1, model=1, family="tbc", actor="player", duration=seconds, records=rows}`. Every row has a strictly increasing integer `seq`, nondecreasing finite `time` within the capture duration, and a fixed `type` schema. Supported types are advice, presentation, retire, attempt, result, damage and damageCoverage. Unknown fields, unsafe tables, malformed ordering and resource-limit failures return a bounded unavailable report without a misleading partial score. The implementation and tests define the exact fields/enums; arbitrary strings, raw GUIDs, names and executable values are not accepted.

Advice includes a numeric epoch, an action classification and (for a spell) its exact ID and expiration. Presentation acknowledges only an earlier current advice sequence. An ordinary player attempt freezes the latest acknowledged, same-epoch spell advice strictly before expiration. Subsequent advice or acknowledgements cannot rescue an unobserved attempt. Retirement/epoch changes make pending attempts unobservable. Results must preserve the attempt identity. Successful ordinary casts are classified as exact matches, different/unmodeled or unobservable; repeated polls, failed casts, channels, pets and duplicate aliases do not create scorable opportunities. Different does not mean incorrect. No observations means unavailable.

Damage is player-only across explicit available-coverage intervals, with four normalized subtype classifications. Readable zero differs from no event; unknown actors, fields, unsupported subtypes and coverage gaps remain visible. Raw logged amount and optional overkill-adjusted amount have separate availability. Rates use covered capture seconds, never invented encounter duration. Pet-inclusive, recipient, role, latency, alternative-action, intent and quality conclusions remain unavailable.

Bounds are 600 seconds, 4,096 records, 1,024 distinct cast aliases, 128 pending casts, 30-second pending age and a 512-KiB deterministic payload charge. The charge is a validation budget, not a precise Lua heap or wire size. Inputs remain unchanged and reports are detached and contain no input aliases or source text. Both Lua runtimes test frozen associations, expiration, retirement, duplicate/conflicting results, partial damage coverage, overflow and every cap.

Live capture/UI, native tuple validation, presentation acknowledgement and role-specific comparisons remain separate pending work. The pure replay foundation does not complete those features.

## Bounded capture builder

`addon.Analytics.NewCapture(options)` creates an in-memory builder for trusted, caller-normalized receipts. Its colon methods are `Start`, `Push`, `Stop`, `Clear` and `GetSnapshot`. `Start` returns a session lease; stale or foreign leases cannot publish evidence. `Push` accepts advice, presentation, retirement, attempt and result receipts. It assigns elapsed times and sequence numbers, keeps raw cast keys private and emits detached numeric aliases. The optional advice maximum age defaults to 0.5 seconds.

`Stop` retires the session before passing its detached trace to Replay. Pending casts remain conservative; later channel or autorepeat classification excludes the entire alias. No provisional score is returned while a capture is active. The final report explicitly states `collection="not-wired"` and does not certify native evidence.

The builder applies the replay duration, row, alias, pending-count and pending-age limits, plus a deterministic 512-KiB payload budget including private keys. Exceeding a limit produces an unavailable report rather than a partial percentage. It installs no event subscriptions, timers, producers, saved data or capture UI. Native tuple validation, active-rotation epochs, presentation acknowledgements and live capture remain pending integration work.

## Native cast-event adapter

`addon.Analytics.NativeCasts` is an explicit, volatile TBC/player diagnostic service with colon methods `Start`, `Stop`, `Clear`, `GetStatus` and `GetSnapshot`. It creates its one owned frame and duration timer only after an explicit Start, records the documented player cast-event prefixes into the capture builder, and stops at world/control boundaries or the requested duration. Source declarations and offline fixtures establish tuple expectations; native acceptance remains unverified.

The adapter reads no target names or optional event tails and emits only numeric cast aliases. Channel observations exclude the whole alias from ordinary-cast scoring. SENT or success without positive ordinary-kind proof remains unmodeled; missing channel events do not imply an ordinary cast. Advice, presentation, ordinary-cast proof, autorepeat association and damage remain unavailable. Session epoch1 is local to the diagnostic, not a rotation epoch.

The outer wrapper identifies the UNIT_SPELLCAST producer while retaining the inner builder's literal not-wired/native-unverified labels. Resource, readability, clock and payload failures retire the session without a partial score. The service retains at most one completed detached capture. Loading it registers no events or timer; runtime advice and presentation hooks remain pending. General → Debugging provides explicit Capture casts (30s), Stop, Cast report and Clear actions independently of pending settings edits. Closing configuration keeps a capture running until its deadline; addon shutdown clears it after cancelling CPU capture and before embedded timers are retired. The copyable report contains at most 200 normalized receipt rows with an explicit omitted count, no private cast keys or target names, and no adherence score.

The report shows the capture's termination or failure token from a fixed allowlist; unfamiliar reasons become `unknown` without copying raw error or payload text. Signal and kind summaries count all retained receipt rows, including duplicates and rows omitted from the 200-row detail view. They are not cast opportunities, distinct actions or scoring denominators. A failed capture has no retained partial receipt summary. Capture seconds are not encounter duration, and rotation attribution remains unavailable. Damage, tank and healer result lines state the missing observations and denominator rather than fabricating zeroes, role assignments or a performance score. This is a diagnostic report, not completion of live analytics or role-specific measurement.

## Capture-only primary advice and native-preview delivery

During the existing explicit TBC cast capture, two fixed startup handoffs arm private local producer sinks in main and the native preview. Off capture, only local nil branches execute at the actual publication/delivery seams; no per-poll observer call, query or trace allocation is added. The actual first selected spell publishes normalized advice independently of glow-message deduplication. No action, rotation disable/error, or preview Hide/Release retires its coverage. Before accepting a native receipt, the collector rechecks the captured current runtime-pass/profile/window witness and any acknowledged visible-delivery witness; dependency/body/window retirement cannot reuse prior coverage. These checks exist only while capture is active.

Only the source method's first real spell icon may acknowledge its strictly earlier advice sequence. Its method/environment and validity source, profile, generation, actual icon identity and readable visibility states must remain current. Samples, unknown display metadata, items, custom/replaced preview methods, hidden/released/reused windows and external glows/WeakAuras cannot produce a positive native-preview acknowledgement. Visible describes RM-owned frame state at that boundary, not human perception or pixel certification. Native acceptance of this new producer remains unverified.

Stop/Clear/shutdown disarm before resource cleanup; capture failures do not retry or suppress gameplay. Reentrant capture/transition/window replacement cannot adopt old evidence. Existing one-frame/one-timer and trace/alias/payload limits remain unchanged. New advice/presentation/retirement rows share the existing bounded trace and200-row copy report; repeated advice refreshes are coverage rows, never opportunities. The builder retains its foundation labels; the adapter's outer producer fields identify the newly wired observations. Ordinary-kind proof remains unavailable and all native cast kinds stay unmodeled, so scoring remains unavailable. No raw GUIDs/names/profile or source keys, rotation attribution, damage, encounter duration or role metrics are added.
