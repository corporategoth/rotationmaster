# Rotation Master help

Rotation Master (RM) is a World of Warcraft addon that highlights the next spell or item to use, right on your action bars. You decide what "next" means with a **rotation**: an ordered list of actions, each with optional **conditions**. RM watches your character and lights up the first action in the list that makes sense right now.

It never presses buttons for you. It only shows you what to press.

## Help inside the game

Every page of the settings window has a gold **?** icon with its own help, and most controls show a short tip when you hover over them. This site collects the same information in one place, with more examples.

> **Tip:** The quickest way to get an answer from other players is the [Rotation Master Discord](https://discord.gg/4aKZSpUMet).
