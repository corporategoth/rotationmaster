# Condition types

These are the built-in condition types, grouped by the category they are placed in on a new profile. You can rearrange categories on the [Conditions](/help/conditions#managing-conditions) tab, so yours may differ.

- **Identifier** is the name used in exports and in Lua; you will see it in shared exports and condition categories.
- **Switch condition by default** marks the types offered in [switch conditions](/help/switching) on a new profile. *Switch only* types are hidden from the ordinary picker by default, because they rarely change during a fight.

Some types depend on optional addons or on your game edition. On editions where the data is restricted, a type can be kept in your rotation but shown as *reference only*; see [Game editions](/help/editions).

Hover a condition in game, or open its **?** icon, for the details of its fields.
